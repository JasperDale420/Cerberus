# Critique: Cerberus Regime Labeling Pipeline

## 1. Are the labels usable as-is?

**The verdict is conditional and answerable in 30 minutes.** The decision splits cleanly on one fact — how the 69 parquets are consumed — so the responsible answer is to do the audit before making operational moves. The 69-file layout is consistent with either per-symbol joining or with dead artifacts from a prior design; both produce the same shape on disk.

What is *established* by the evidence pack, independent of consumption:

1. **The per-symbol vol column is severely miscalibrated against any plausible interpretation of "market volatility regime."** Median symbol spends 94.3% of days HIGH-or-SHOCK; only 3 of 68 names reach LOW on ≥5% of days; aggregate 0.32% LOW vs. 67.6% HIGH/SHOCK against a SPY-truth distribution of ~9%/~80%/~18%/~2%. This is a fact about the column, not a claim about its use.
2. **Train and live use different estimators** (SMA + absolute thresholds vs. Hurst + EWMA z) with no documented invariant tying them and no standard report comparing them on common dates.
3. **The Risk axis is silently absent from training data and silently present in live gating.** Strategies whose `activation:` keys on `RISK_ON` condition on a signal autoresearch never observed.
4. **Reported 81.9% accuracy is in-sample** — same SPY periods tune and score.

What is *not yet* established:

- Whether autoresearch joins per-symbol parquets, joins only the SPY parquet, or treats `vol_regime` as a feature versus a filter.
- Whether the SPY ground-truth proportions used for the 81.9% were independently labeled or generated by the labeler's own threshold scheme. Without this the accuracy is *at least* in-sample; whether also tautological is open.
- Whether 98.6% HIGH on TSLA carries useful conditional information in the residual ~3.6 days/yr — rare and uninformative are not the same; mutual information against forward returns settles it.

**For the labels themselves:** mark already-shipped findings conditioning on `vol_regime` for non-SPY symbols as *provisional* in the results store until the audit and an OOS spot-check land. Reversible, cheap, proportionate.

## 2. Highest-leverage fix

**The highest-leverage *action* is the audit (P0). The highest-leverage *object* is the ground truth, not the labeler. Conditional on confirming per-symbol joining, the lead redesign is option (a) plus a co-published market-state column — but only after held-out OOS truth exists to choose between candidate calibrations.**

- **(a) Per-symbol percentile thresholds.** Directly fixes Findings 1 and 3: defensive ETF and meme stock both reach LOW at the same percentile of their own history — what "low vol for this asset" should mean. The objection that this "silently redefines HIGH 68 ways" only holds without a written contract. What it loses is the cross-symbol market signal — that all single-name z-scores spiked together. A co-published market-state column (one shared value per date, from VIX or SPY 30d realized vol against absolute thresholds calibrated to that benchmark's own distribution) recovers it.
- **(b) Replace Labeler A with Labeler B.** Train/live convergence is valuable only if B is validated. Hurst on daily bars over a short window is noisy and regime-dependent. Adopting B without OOS validation trades one unvalidated estimator for another and removes the only diagnostic available — A-vs-B divergence. Convergence is the destination, not the next step.
- **(c) Add Risk axis to training data.** Necessary, but does nothing about vol-axis miscalibration. Independent; ship in parallel.
- **(d) Build defensible OOS multi-asset ground truth.** Both A and B are unscored against held-out multi-asset truth. Until that exists, "which labeler is better" cannot be answered, only debated. P1 unlocks every downstream decision.

On framing: applied to SPY, the absolute thresholds (8/20/50) are not a category error — SPY's distribution is the implicit reference. Applied to TSLA they are wrong *for TSLA's distribution*, not wrong as a concept. The right architecture keeps absolute thresholds for a market-level column calibrated to its own benchmark, and uses percentile thresholds for the per-symbol column. Document in `REGIME_CONTRACT.md` next to the labeler.

## 3. Hidden assumptions that might invalidate the critique

1. **Consumption pattern (load-bearing).** Per-symbol join translates calibration findings into training distortion; SPY-only join collapses the verdict to "delete unused parquets and document the contract." Resolve by reading the autoresearch driver and `src/agent/`.
2. **Filter mode vs. feature mode.** `df[df.vol_regime == "HIGH"]` and `groupby(regime)` reporting inherit miscalibration in full. A tree model fed `vol_regime` as a feature *can* attenuate noise — but can also exploit the constant-feature × symbol-id interaction, turning miscalibration into a symbol-class proxy by leakage. Same audit records both.
3. **81.9% accuracy construction.** If SPY truth was independently human-labeled, in-sample tuning is the only critique. If generated by the same threshold scheme being scored, the metric tests SMA-vs-rule-generator agreement, not whether the rules describe market physics. Evidence pack is silent; verify before promoting circularity from suspicion to claim.
4. **Information content of rare bins.** 98.6% HIGH leaves ~3.6 days/yr in other bins. Whether that residual carries actionable conditional return information is empirical (MI of `vol_regime` with forward 1-day return on TSLA, vs. permutation null). Measure before declaring "uninformative."
5. **Risk-axis 58% baseline.** Meaningful only relative to class balance. If RISK_ON is ~80% of days, 58% is *below* always-predict-RISK_ON. Recompute against actual class balance and constant-classifier baseline before treating as the punchline.
6. **Strategies actually gated on broken states.** Without auditing `activation:` blocks in `config/strategies.yaml`, the *specific* harm of LOW-vol blindness is not yet tied to a named strategy. The general harm — autoresearch cannot learn what it cannot observe — stands.

## 4. Concrete dangers

1. **Symbol-class skew presented as regime conditioning** (conditional on per-symbol joining). With most non-SPY symbols ~constant in HIGH/SHOCK, `groupby(vol_regime)` for those names approximates partition by symbol identity. Reports labeled "edge in HIGH regime" describe the unconditional return of a symbol class. Autoresearch learns *symbol selection* while reporting *regime conditioning*.
2. **HIGH-regime claims on non-SPY names are nearly equivalent to unconditional claims.** "Outperforms in HIGH on TSLA" is mechanically promoting on TSLA's unconditional return for ~98.6% of the sample.
3. **LOW-regime strategies cannot be evaluated on 65 of 68 symbols.** Mean reversion, theta selling, carry — edges concentrating in low realized vol — have no LOW observations across most of the universe. Looks like null edge; is missing data. Wrong-direction error in the promotion pipeline.
4. **Train/live divergence is silent and unbounded.** Detectable only if a comparison report exists. None does.
5. **Risk-axis gating on a near-noise classifier.** Autoresearch never trained against it, so live conditional edge is unmeasured. Backtest edge is unconditional-on-risk; live edge conditional on near-noise. No standard report surfaces this gap.
6. **Reported accuracy may be self-consistent rather than informative.** If truth derives from the same threshold scheme, 81.9% cannot detect whether the scheme is wrong. Any uncertainty estimate built on it is mis-specified beneath naive overfitting.

## Prioritized fix list

1. **P0 — 30-minute consumption audit.** Read the autoresearch driver, `src/agent/`, and config resolution. Record (i) per-symbol vs. SPY-only join, (ii) feature vs. filter use, (iii) every `activation:` block keyed on `vol_regime` or `risk_state`. Gates everything below.
2. **P0 — Verify the 81.9% ground truth construction.** Read `optimized_params.json`, the labeling script, any SPY truth file. Confirms whether truth is independent or threshold-derived.
3. **P0 conditional — Mark non-SPY regime-conditioned findings provisional.** Metadata flag in the results store; do not block live promotions on a flag alone. Fires only if (1) confirms per-symbol joining; lifted when P1 spot-checks land.
4. **P1 — Multi-asset OOS ground truth.** Hand-label 4-6 held-out periods (JNJ, TSLA, sector ETF, commodity ETF, plus SPY periods *not* in the original 8). Score A *and* B against this set. Until this exists, picking a labeler is vibes.
5. **P1 — Information-content tests on per-symbol `vol_regime`.** MI of bin assignment with forward 1-day return by symbol, vs. permutation null. Distinguishes informative-but-rare from truly uninformative.
6. **P1 — Risk axis: ship a real classifier or stop emitting it.** Recompute 58% against class balance and constant-classifier baseline. Either re-derive a proxy that meaningfully beats baseline on hand-labeled stress windows (Mar 2020, Jan 2022, SVB Mar 2023, yen-carry Aug 2024 as RISK_OFF on t±2; ≥80% of unambiguous calm as RISK_ON), or remove the axis and have `RISK_ON`-gated strategies treat it as constant. Currently the worst of both worlds.
7. **P2 — Redesign Labeler A: market-state column + per-symbol z-score column.** Co-published, documented thresholds; estimator selected by P1 ground-truth performance. Same estimator in train and live. Re-emit all 69 parquets with no silent alias of old `vol_regime`.
8. **P2 — Document the regime contract.** `REGIME_CONTRACT.md` next to the labeler: axes, estimators, threshold derivation, hysteresis, train/live invariant. Methodology drift is the symptom; absent contract is the disease.
9. **P3 — Aggregate-distribution CI checks on every labeler emit.** Fail build if (i) market-state column disagrees across symbols on any common date, (ii) >50% of universe spends >80% of days in any single bin of the per-symbol column, (iii) train and live disagree on the market-state column for any common date in the last 30 days. Universe-aggregate only — per-symbol forced distributions would false-positive on a genuinely-quiet utility ETF.
